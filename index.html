About Me

I am a senior undergraduate at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, majoring in Applied Mathematics (Data Science) and pursuing a double degree in Economics at the National School of Development. I spent Spring 2026 as a visiting student at UC Berkeley.

My research interests are in machine learning and optimization theory. I am fortunate to work with Prof. Yuejie Chi at Yale on efficient reasoning transformers, and Prof. David Simchi-Levi at MIT on LLM agents and multi-agent coordination. Previously, I had the pleasure of working with Prof. Kun Yuan at Peking University on communication-efficient stochastic optimization.

I am applying for PhD programs starting in Fall 2027. Please feel free to get in touch!
